Flagship programme

Power of Play

Measured through the Divergent Association Task and The LEGO Foundation's Holistic Skills framework, tracking creative thinking, play frequency and core cognitive and physical skills — in children and in the teachers who deliver alongside them.

100%increase in children's creative thinking skills (DAT)
300%increase in children's frequency of play
92%increase in teachers' creative thinking skills (DAT)

Skills growth

The LEGO Foundation's Holistic Skills framework, 2024.

47%growth in Problem-Solving (Cognitive Skills)
53%growth in Build and Make (Physical Skills)
38%growth in Staying Focused (Cognitive Skills)

Skills children identify in themselves

Qualitative

Teamwork, Creativity, Craftsmanship, Motivation, Trial & Error, Confidence, Computing skills, Kindness.

School sessions

Day of Play

Single-day sessions measured with The LEGO Foundation's Holistic Skills framework, capturing how children feel about the day itself — whether it was fun, whether they'd try it again, and whether it gave them a chance to play and build with others.

93%agreed or strongly agreed Day of Play was fun
87%agreed or strongly agreed they'd give Day of Play a try
77%agreed Day of Play let them play with others
64%agreed they felt curious about Day of Play

What children said

Feedback gathered directly from children after their Day of Play session, 2024.

Why was learning through play important?

Child feedback
  • “It teaches you to work together and it taught me how to code things.”
  • “We need a break from learning and to be creative and just express our imagination freely.”

What's the best thing that happened in the Day of Play?

Child feedback
  • “When we accidentally made a mistake in our building and we had to remake it, and it turned out better.”
  • “The best thing was when my partner and I built something together, and it actually worked!”
  • “I made a new friend, and we built a project together.”

STEAM Live and related

STEAM Programmes

The broadest toolkit of any programme — the Divergent Association Task, the 8 Core Competencies framework, the Millennium Cohort Study, Net Promoter Score and MicroBit Foundation coding-skills measures, tracking creative thinking, career aspirations and coding confidence.

83%of STEAM Live classrooms saw an increase in creative thinking (DAT)
25%of students raised their occupational aspirations
45%could imagine a job of the future not yet invented
60NPS score — teachers recommending STEAM Live

Coding confidence & competencies

2023 cohort.

85%said the micro:bit made them think they could be creative with coding
62%said the micro:bit made them think anyone can code
54%had mentioned iOi to a family member

Top 3 competencies (8 Core Competencies framework): Creativity (76%), Collaboration (63%), Communication (53%). Teachers separately ranked Creativity, Curiosity and Communication as the top three skills they saw shift after the iOi intervention.

New STEAM career aspirations

  • Astronaut, Surgeon, Mathematician
  • Game Designer, LEGO Architect, Robotics Creator

Working with teachers

Creative Sparks

A teacher-facing programme — the evidence so far is entirely qualitative, drawn directly from teacher feedback about what changes in their own classroom practice and in how their students engage.
